# Accessibility Statement

Musterbetrieb GmbH is committed to making the website Musterbetrieb accessible in
accordance with the Act of 26 April 2024 on ensuring compliance with the accessibility
requirements of certain products and services by economic operators (Journal of Laws 2024,
item 731), which transposes Directive (EU) 2019/882 (the European Accessibility Act) into
Polish law. It has been in force since 28 June 2025.

This accessibility statement applies to https://example.at.

## Enforcement procedure

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can report the lack of accessibility to the
President of the Management Board of the State Fund for Rehabilitation of Disabled Persons
(PFRON). The President receives reports about every product and service the Act covers, and
either deals with a report or passes it to the competent market surveillance authority,
which for e-commerce services is the minister responsible for digital affairs.
